I’m Arshad Mehmood. I work on backend software at SAP SE in Germany, mostly with Go and Kubernetes. I have an M.Sc. in Business Informatics from the University of Mannheim. Long before that, I was a computer science student in Islamabad trying to figure out how to write an app for my HTC Google G1.

## My first Android apps

My first app converted temperatures and showed the result in a little Android pop-up. The first one I published solved a problem I had during Ramadan. I wanted my phone to tell me when to start and break my fast. It earned $2.07 in its first month. I still remember the amount.

I wrote [the longer story](/personal/the-most-fulfilling-2-i-made-and-my-android-developer-journey/) years later. I went on to found [Kookydroid](https://play.google.com/store/apps/dev?id=7044232667654327190) and publish more Android apps. You can find those and some of my client work in my [portfolio](/portfolio/).

## What I’m working on

Outside work I run my own services and build tools for things I do often. One of those is [Vault Linker](https://github.com/arshad115/vault-linker). I had hundreds of notes in Obsidian and wanted to link them without going through each one by hand. It now runs from the command line or inside Obsidian.

## Why I write

Sometimes I just want to find the same command again six months later. Those notes go into [Today I Learned](/today-i-learned/). When the interesting part is how I got from an error message to a fix, I write a [post](/posts/). My [Fail2ban lockout](/devops/how-fail2ban-locked-me-out-of-my-own-server-traefik-authentik/) is one of those stories.
