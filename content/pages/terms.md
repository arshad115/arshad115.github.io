## Privacy

I do not sell visitor data and I do not run ads on this site.

### Hosting

The site is static files on GitHub Pages. GitHub’s servers see the usual request metadata (IP address, user agent, referrer) in access logs. I do not run Universal Analytics or a marketing pixel.

### Comments

Comments on posts use [Giscus](https://giscus.app), which is GitHub Discussions. GitHub’s privacy policy applies when you leave a comment while signed in to GitHub. TIL notes do not have comments.

### Contact form

The [contact form](/contact/) asks for your name, email address, an optional website, and your message so that I can receive and reply to your enquiry. A Cloudflare Worker validates the submission and forwards it to [Web3Forms](https://web3forms.com/privacy), operated by Web3Creative; the Web3Forms access key is never sent to your browser. Cloudflare processes the request metadata needed to operate and protect the endpoint. Web3Forms processes the submitted fields and delivery metadata and may use spam-filtering providers. Submissions are delivered to me by email and may be retained by Web3Forms for up to three years unless deleted sooner. Data may be processed outside the European Economic Area under the safeguards described in Web3Forms' privacy policy and [Data Processing Agreement](https://web3forms.com/dpa).

Submitting the form is voluntary. The information is processed only to handle and reply to your message. Do not send passwords, financial information, health information, or other sensitive data through the form. You can ask for access, correction, or deletion through the same [contact form](/contact/).

### Search

Search runs in your browser against a Pagefind index of the public HTML. Queries do not go to a third-party search SaaS.

### Cookies

The theme preference (`light` / `dark`) is stored in `localStorage` on your device. There is no login cookie for the site itself.

## Disclosure

I am not running affiliate programs or AdSense on this site. If that changes, this page will say so.

If you have a privacy question, [write to me](/contact/).
